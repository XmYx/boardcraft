# BoardCraft app icon

`boardcraft.svg` is a **draft** icon (a heron standing in a storyboard frame above a panel strip),
in the suite's three-colour style: Ink `#0b0b0c`, Paper `#f2f1ee` and the BoardCraft colour
`#e26842`, on a 512 tile with `rx=112` and the art clipped at `rx=106`.

The other Craft apps' icons are engraved creatures drawn by the ArtCraft owner; this draft is a
stand-in until the ArtCraft team supplies BoardCraft's. The rendered sizes the packaging needs
(`boardcraft-1024.png`, `.ico`, `.icns`, `hicolor/{16..512}/apps/ai.storyteller.boardcraft.png`)
are not committed yet; generate them from the final SVG.
